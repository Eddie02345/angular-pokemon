Kanto Region

Known as the pioneer region of modern Pokémon training and industrial technological expansion. Centered around Silph Co. and the prestigious Indigo Plateau, Kanto established the original eight-gym certification system to test battle fundamentals, type adaptation, and raw discipline across urban and coastal terrains.

Johto Region

Situated directly west of Kanto, Johto is deeply rooted in ancient folklore, architectural heritage, and legendary myths. From the historic Bell Tower of Ecruteak to the seaside trade port of Olivine, Johto’s Gym Leaders emphasize patience, elemental harmony, and ancestral battle strategies under the Silver Conference.
